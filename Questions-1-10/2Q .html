Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common booking questions.

How do I book a room?

Select your dates, choose the room type, enter guest details, and confirm the reservation.

Can I modify my booking?

This demonstration permits modification at least three days before arrival, subject to room availability.

Can I cancel a reservation?

Eligible bookings can be cancelled at least three days before arrival under the demonstration policy.

Does this website process online payments?

No. A real deployment needs a secure payment gateway and server-side payment verification.

Will I receive a confirmation email?

This frontend does not send emails. An email service must be connected to the backend to deliver booking confirmations.
